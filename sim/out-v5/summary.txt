=== 0. Rules, storage and gas model, player kinds ===
Petri economy simulator v5 (sim/src/main.rs) on petri-core; parameters loaded from ../contract/launch-rules.json
  128x128 dish, 15000 ms generations (5760 a day); feed_ppm 3, keeper_bps 200, reach 8 on stake decaying to 8750 bps each tide, burn_bps 5000, payback 480,
  min_cell_price 0.5 PETRI, live_floor 1000, petri_price 0.0002 NEAR, max_supply 1000000 PETRI, min_join 0.01 NEAR, cell_fee 0.0001 NEAR, tide_every 240, tide_bps 1000,
  tide_bounty_bps 100 (max 0.001 NEAR), sell_bps 5000, sell_cap_bps 1000, siege_x 3, buffer 3, max_plant_cells 64, max_catchup 120, max_lag 480, house 5 strains.
Storage (borsh layout of what each call writes, +40 B per record; 1e19 yocto per byte):
  join 595 + 3L + 2N bytes incl. the 140 B prepaid purchase entry (wallet L=16: 667 B = 0.00667 NEAR; guest L=32: 715 B); buy 140 B;
  plant_deposit = (220 + 4c (+ note + 64)) B + 0.0001 NEAR per cell; stored 162 + 4c B (+8 the first time, + 49 + note), the rest of the deposit
  goes to the vault (or credit pool); sell 104 B from the proceeds; tide entry + index 271 B from the vault; credit record 73 + L + Ls B (guest 124 B,
  from the sponsorship); a Credit record a tide creates for a winner without one, Skip entries and board growth in catch-ups are paid by the contract.
Gas (estimate from NEAR's fee schedule; 1 TGas = 0.0001 NEAR): call 2.5 TGas + 60 Ggas per storage op + 5.6/63 Mgas per byte read/written
  (the per-colony arrays are rewritten whole: 24 B per colony per catch-up, 16 B per colony per tide, 40 B per colony per join) + 0.82 Mgas per
  wasm op (a generation ~20k + 60 x live + 50 x colonies alive ops) + 3 TGas per NEAR payout; each credit-pool winner at a tide costs
  263 Ggas (its Colony and Credit records are read and rewritten). A transaction may burn 300 TGas; a catch-up stops after the
  generation that crosses 160 TGas. Wallet players pay their gas (it counts in cash); the relayer pays the guests'.

Player kinds (NEAR brought at join / NEAR willing to attach in all; winnings, sales and bounties go back into the wallet):
  Builder 0.05/0.25 still lifes near home | Raider 0.05/0.25 gliders/LWSS at rivals, 1 move in 3 a blinker siege | Defender 0.05/0.25
  eaters/beehives | Methuselah 0.1/0.5 an R-pentomino an hour within 40 cells | Late 0.05/0.25 builder joining day 2-3 | Whale 2/3 one
  colony, pulsars/beehives anywhere | SplitWhale the same 2/3 over 10 colonies (20 from 1,500 players) | Sniper 0.05/0.25 plants in the 3
  generations before each tide, sells after each | Locker 1.5/2 a 16x16 lattice of blocks 8 apart (every empty cell in its buffer),
  repairs every 20 gens, plants renewal blocks when its cap < its live cells | Racer 0.01 never plants, advances for pot + bounty |
  Seller 0.2 buys and sells back | Guest: an ordinary relayer guest, 0.05 NEAR of sponsored credit, plays like a builder |
  SybilGuest: one operator = a wallet builder (0.05/0.25) + 10 relayer guests near it (0.05 credit each) that plant blocks, sell
  every tide and claim | SelfSponsor: one operator sponsors 10 own full-access sub-accounts with 0.05 NEAR each, plays them on credit,
  claims their NEAR, stops spending credit a day before the end and unsponsors them (credit won included) | House: the 5 strains,
  0.05 + 0.02 top-ups, 1 NEAR budget each, notes, no siege, every 50-140 gens. The team keeper (unregistered) advances when 20
  behind or the pot holds 5 PETRI (agents/run.mjs). Careful players spend at most half their PETRI per planting, try 30 spots before
  paying for a siege. After each tide sellers race for the allowance: bots first, then a random quarter of the rest.
  A relayer guest's key is a function-call key for join/buy/plant/sell/claim/advance only: the NEAR it is paid (tide claims, sales,
  bounties) lands in the guest account and cannot be moved, so it is not cash for anyone ('stranded').
