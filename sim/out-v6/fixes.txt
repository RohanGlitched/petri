PETRI v6: RULES THAT STILL LOOK BROKEN, THE CHANGE THAT FIXES EACH, AND ITS MEASURED EFFECT
(sim/out-v6; every experiment re-runs the same seeds with one change; the measured tables follow below. contract/, core/, wasm/,
web/ and agents/ were not changed: these are proposals.)

F1. Tide entries eat a young dish's tides. Each tide that runs stores 289 B (0.00289 NEAR) from its 5% share: 39% of all tide pay
    with one outsider, 31% with five, 8% with twenty.
    Change: keep only the history checkpoint on chain per tide (~120 B: generation + chain) and put pay/bounty/paid/as_credit/
    credit_pay/credit_paid in the event the replayers already read; or run tides less often (tide_every 960) while the dish is young.
    Measured (sim switch small_tide_entry / --set tide_every=960): tide storage 39% -> 18% (120 B) or 15% (960); Penny alone
    +0.44 -> +0.65 / +0.60; Methuselah among 5 +0.07 -> +0.19 / +0.31, among 20 +0.27 -> +0.25 / +0.65. Rarer tides leave more in
    the vault at the end (0.18-0.69 NEAR). Tide bps 200 is worse (storage 50% of pay).

F2. The house subsidy goes to whoever is alone on the dish. A 0.01 NEAR Penny alone took 100% of 164 tides and +0.44 NEAR of the
    house's 2.3 NEAR in a week. This is the stated design ("the vault keeps what the house paid in for the first real player"), so
    it is a judgment call, not a bug; if it is unwanted:
    Change: cap one colony's share of a tide (tide_cap_bps 2500: no colony takes more than 25% of a tide; the rest stays in the vault).
    Measured: Penny alone +0.44 -> +0.28 and 0.07 NEAR stays in the vault; with 5 or 20 outsiders no measurable change.

F3. Slide-royalty farming pays. A farmer minting 8 common patterns at launch earned 6,296 PETRI of royalties in a week and +0.29
    NEAR cash (150 players); ordinary players lost 0.01-0.12 more than with no slides.
    Change: slide_royalty_bps 0 (keep the 25% licence discount, which is the same for everyone), or pay the royalty to the agar,
    or only to the slide's minter for plantings within N cells of the minting colony.
    Measured (slide_royalty_bps 0): farmer +0.29 -> -0.20; licensed plantings unchanged (7,852 -> 8,374).

F4. Free play can still reach NEAR through royalties. A colony that never attached NEAR (a relayer guest) pays royalties in PETRI to
    a slide's holder, who can sell them for vault NEAR: ~56 PETRI (~0.006 NEAR) a week per operator with 10 free guests.
    Change (contract plant()): when the planter has never attached NEAR (`!books(id).attached`), the royalty goes to the agar.
    Measured (royalty_guest_to_agar): royalties from credit-only planters 56 -> 0 PETRI; operator cash -0.158 -> -0.151 (noise).

F5. Gas grows with the colonies that have stake, and stake lingers ~60 tides after a colony's last planting. On an active 30,000-
    colony dish up to 27,500 rows were hot; the largest plant/buy/claim were 237/234/233 TGas and 16,102 calls passed the 200 TGas
    the site prepays; above ~21,000 hot rows a 200 TGas call steps only one generation.
    Changes: (a) web/src/lib/wallet.ts: prepay 300 TGas for plant, buy, sell and join (unused gas is refunded); (b) contract
    books.rs flush_idle: let a row leave once its stake is below STAKE_UNIT / reach (125), where its pay cap is under one cell and it
    earns nothing anyway (identical feed); (c) lib.rs catch_up_by: reserve only the feed part (26 B a row) for the store unless a
    tide falls inside the catch-up window, since the purse part is written only by tides and purse changes.
    Measured: (b) calls over 200 TGas 16,102 -> 28 at 30,000 colonies (max hot rows 27,538 -> 26,080; plant max 237 -> 231 TGas);
    economics unchanged within seed noise at 150 players. (c) generations per 200 TGas call at 25,000 and 30,000 rows: 1 -> 120
    (analytic). (a) is needed regardless: the analytic plant is 221 TGas at 30,000 rows.

F6. The keeper pot is stranded on mainnet. Only `advance` pays it, the keeper and house never take it, and outsiders rarely call
    advance: 1,480 PETRI sat in the pot after a week on the young 20-player dish (12 PETRI at 150 players, where racers exist).
    Change (lib.rs): pay the pot to any non-house caller whose call ran at least one generation (plant, join, buy, sell, claim).
    Measured (pot_any_call, 150 players): pot left 12.3 -> 2.2 PETRI; the racer's loss -0.019 -> -0.006; builders unchanged.

F7. The testnet keeper spins (agents/run.mjs line 467). If the keeper is registered it advances whenever the pot holds over 50 PETRI,
    but v6 never pays it the pot, so the pot never drains: 4,820 advances a day, 1.7 NEAR/day at mainnet gas price (~17 testnet
    NEAR/day at testnet's 10x price), pot stuck at 461 PETRI.
    Change: drop `(keeperRegistered && Number(st.keeper_pot) > 50_000_000)` from the testnet condition.
    Measured: 4,820 -> 30 advances a day; gas 1.73 -> 0.016 NEAR/day.

F8. The mainnet house's NEAR lasts ~5-6 days at 0.55 NEAR per strain (1/8 pace, 100 PETRI/day, 0.12 floor): ~0.08 NEAR per strain
    per day. To keep playing through judging (Nov 18, ~14 days from relaunch) each strain needs ~1.2 NEAR, or PETRI_PACE 16 and
    PETRI_DAILY_PETRI 50 (linear estimate from these runs, not simulated). The docs' "about 1.1 NEAR a strain a week" is v5 testnet
    pace; at mainnet pace a strain spent ~0.46 NEAR before running dry.

Not broken (checked): house laundering (all paths refused; 0 PETRI moved in 343 runs), sybil guests (no NEAR in guest accounts,
operators lose 0.14-0.16), self-sponsoring (-0.57..-0.72: the grant can only be reclaimed unspent, won credit is spent first), the
carpet locker (loses at every size), the dormancy skip and idle-tide skips (no storage spent on empty dishes), the join's O(1) cost
on a current dish.
