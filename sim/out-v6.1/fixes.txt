PETRI v6.1: EACH FIX FROM sim/out-v6 AND ITS MEASURED EFFECT, AND THE RULES THAT STILL LOOK BROKEN
(sim/out-v6.1, re-run on v6.1 part 2: the kept rule and pacts; every comparison re-runs the same seeds with v6's rules and layout set back
('V6' scenarios, which reproduce sim/out-v6), or with one v6.1 change taken out ('NOKEPT' = without the kept rule); the measured tables
follow below.)

PART 2 (v6.1 part 2, measured here)

P1 (free play reaching NEAR) FIXED. Rule: PETRI earned before a colony's first NEAR stays in the dish. A guest cannot send PETRI and takes
    no keeper pot; when it first attaches NEAR, what it earned, bought with credit or was paid in royalties is kept PETRI, and so is PETRI
    anyone buys with credit; plantings and slides spend kept PETRI first; while a colony holds any it cannot sell for NEAR, send PETRI or pay
    for a pact. Testnet 150 p + 3 SybilAttach + 3 SybilTransfer operators (each 10 relayer guests), 7 d, 6 seeds:
    - exit (a), guests ft_transfer their PETRI to one wallet that sells it: without the rule 477 PETRI moved per operator and the vault paid
      +0.047 NEAR (+0.043..+0.053) a week per operator for it; with the rule every transfer is refused (0 moved, 0 NEAR).
    - exit (b), guests farm a day, attach 0.01 NEAR each and sell what they farmed: with the rule 0 NEAR (82 sales refused per operator,
      292 PETRI still kept at the end); without it +0.0008 NEAR per operator, because a guest's day of farming (about 30 PETRI, 0.003 NEAR)
      is worth less than a sale's own record and refund reserve (0.0035 NEAR), so most of its sales never happen; the 0.01 NEAR attach is
      a loss either way (cash -0.055 with the rule, -0.044 without).
    - every colony in the run: vault NEAR paid for PETRI earned before its first NEAR (or bought with credit, or moved in by a guest's
      transfer) 0.0000 with the rule, 0.167 without. The keeper pot no longer reaches guests (left at the end 1.3 vs 1.6 PETRI).
    - cost to everyone else, mainnet 150 p 7 d: within seed noise (Builder -0.187 vs -0.190, Methuselah +0.350 vs +0.368, Whale -2.007 vs
      -1.948, SelfSponsor -0.597 vs -0.620; the young dishes are identical, no credit there).
P2 (pacts, new). A builder that pays the raider nearest it (within 40 cells) 2 PETRI a tide window for a pact (pact_radius 8) changes the
    raider's game: 150 p 7 d, 12 pacts a builder, raids aimed elsewhere 2.0 and plantings refused by the contract 1.6 per raider; young 20:
    6 pacts, 1.8 aimed elsewhere, 0.8 refused. At that naive price it does not pay the builder: cash -0.222 vs -0.187 at 150 p (the PETRI
    and the 0.0038 NEAR of storage a pact are spent, and PETRI paid is PETRI not planted: 128 k vs 163 k cell-generations). Pacts are worth
    it only where a raider's threat is real, which is what the strains' council prices with its model.

STILL BROKEN, OR NEW

N1. BY DESIGN, with the numbers. The pulsar's first minter collects 10% of every licensed planting of it: +0.11 NEAR (+0.08..+0.15) a
    week at 150 players, 3,171 PETRI of royalties from 466 whale plantings. Who pays: only planters that choose the licence (plant's `slide`
    is optional, and the royalty is PETRI from their own balance, `toll`), and they still come out ahead: whales -1.97 with the farmer vs
    -2.01 with no farmer and -2.05 with no licence at all (licence_min_cells 49); with royalty 0 they would keep the whole 25% (-1.82).
    The house never pays (it plants only slides it holds) and a guest's royalty goes to the agar. The vault pays the farmer only through
    sell-backs, at the capped half price any holder gets, and its totals hardly move with the royalty: sales 10.23 NEAR with the farmer vs
    10.16 with royalty 0 (9.51 with no licence), tide shares 29.30 vs 29.09 (29.49). What moves the vault is the licence's discount
    itself (less PETRI burned and returned, so more to sell), which every licence has whoever holds it. Tide winners shift within seed
    noise (Methuselah +0.28 with the farmer, +0.31 royalty 0, +0.34 no licence, +0.35 no farmer; ranges overlap). So the farmer's gain is
    the licence working as designed (the first to fossilize a large pattern holds its licence), not a leak of house or vault money;
    licence_min_cells stays 12. If it should not pay, licence_min_cells 49 or slide_royalty_bps 0 make the farmer lose 0.16.
F2 (judgment; tide_cap_bps 2500 measured and not adopted). The house's subsidy still goes to whoever is alone on the dish, and more of it
    reaches it now that tides store less: a 0.01 NEAR Penny alone nets +0.57 (v6 +0.44) of the house's 2.28 NEAR in a week. tide_cap_bps
    2500 (no colony more than 25% of a tide) was to be added if it cost real players under 0.02 NEAR a week in every scenario and removed
    most of the lone farmer's gain. It does neither: the lone Penny keeps +0.41 of +0.57 (28% removed, 0.15 NEAR left idle in the vault),
    and with 5 outsiders it costs the Methuselah 0.055 a week (+0.061 -> +0.006), the one outsider that plays well early, which the
    "first players should be rewarded" goal protects (with 20 outsiders it gains, +0.21 -> +0.24). The first player taking the subsidy
    while alone is that goal at work; a cap mostly strands NEAR. tide_every 960: +0.61, 0.22 left; tide_bps 200: +0.46. Left as is.
F8 (house runway). The mainnet house runs dry on day 7 (6..7) at 0.55 NEAR a strain, 1/8 pace, 100 PETRI a day (v6: day 6): about 1.2 NEAR
    a strain would last to Nov 18 (linear estimate, not simulated).
N2 (note). The methuselah stays the one kind ahead at 15 and 150 players (+0.38, +0.37; v6 +0.42, +0.41). It loses at 1,500 players
    (-0.05) and over 30 days (-0.39).
Now modelled (P1): guests' PETRI moved by ft_transfer to a wallet that sells it, and guests that attach a little NEAR to sell what they
    farmed. Both pay 0 vault NEAR under the kept rule.

Not broken (checked again on v6.1 part 2): house laundering (all paths refused; 0 PETRI moved in 467 runs), sybil guests (no NEAR in guest
accounts; operators lose 0.10-0.12 at the median), self-sponsoring (-0.51..-0.72 at the median), the carpet locker (loses at every size), dormancy and idle-tide skips,
the join's cost on a current dish (3.6 TGas analytic, 60 TGas at most when it caught up through a paying tide), the 10,000-row cap
(never reached at 30,000 colonies).
