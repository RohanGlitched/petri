Petri economy simulator v3 (sim/src/main.rs), petri-core rules, 128x128 wrapped dish, 15 s generations (5,760 a day).
Launch rules: feed_ppm 10, keeper_bps 200, burn_bps 5000, payback 480, min_cell_price 0.5 PETRI, live_floor 1000, petri_price 0.0002 NEAR, max_supply 1,000,000 PETRI,
  min_join 0.01 NEAR, cell_fee 0.0001 NEAR, tide_every 240, tide_bps 5000, tide_bounty_bps 100, sell_bps 5000, sell_cap_bps 1000, buffer 3, max_plant_cells 64.
Estimates (not measured on chain): join storage 632 B = 0.00632 NEAR of every join stake; purchase record 110 B; a planting attaches
  plant_deposit = (300 + 6/cell) B + 0.0001 NEAR/cell (+ note + 64 B), the contract keeps what it stores, est. (146 + 6/cell) B (+ note + 49 B),
  and the rest goes to the vault; sale record 105 B from the proceeds; tide entry 240 B from the vault (from the contract when the vault is empty);
  gas 3.5 TGas per call + 0.3 TGas per generation it catches up + 2 TGas when it sends NEAR, at 0.0001 NEAR per TGas.

Columns, per player of each kind (a sybil whale is one player with many colonies), NEAR unless marked:
  paid      = everything attached: join stakes, purchases and planting deposits, storage included
  tides     = NEAR won at tides plus tide bounties; sold = NEAR received for sell-backs (after each sale's record)
  held@sell = PETRI still held at the end, valued at the sell-back price (paper: see exit liquidity)
  net       = tides + sold + held@sell - paid, as min / median / max over seeds of the kind's mean player
  cash med  = tides + sold - paid (what actually came back in NEAR), median
  gas       = estimated gas the player burned (not in net); net-gas = net minus it
  back/1    = (tides + sold + held@sell) / paid; PETRI e/s = PETRI earned from the feed / PETRI spent planting (PETRI, not NEAR)
  feed%     = the kind's share of all PETRI the agar fed (all players of the kind together)
  capped%   = share of the kind's living cell-generations not paid because of the reach cap

Player kinds:
  Builder 0.05 NEAR, still lifes near home | Raider 0.05, gliders/LWSS aimed at rivals, 1 in 3 moves a blinker dropped on a rival (siege)
  Defender 0.05, eaters/beehives | Methuselah 0.1, one R-pentomino an hour (growth bet) | Late 0.05, builder joining day 2-3
  Whale 2 NEAR in one colony, pulsars/beehives anywhere, as often as the sybil's colonies together | SybilWhale the same 2 NEAR split over 10 colonies (20 at 1,500 players)
  Sniper 0.05, plants only in the 3 generations before each tide, sells after each | Locker 1.5 NEAR, lays a lattice of 256 blocks
    8 cells apart at launch so every empty cell is in its buffer, repairs it every 20 gens | Racer 0.01, never plants, calls advance
    just before the team keeper would and right after each tide when pot + bounty beat the gas, sells the pot | Seller 0.2, buys
    and sells back as fast as the allowance allows | House: the 5 team strains, 0.05 NEAR + 0.02 top-ups, notes, no tide share.
  NEAR wallets: builders/raiders/defenders/late/sniper 0.25 NEAR in all, methuselah 0.5, whale 3, sybil whale 3 split, locker 2;
  tide winnings (claimed daily), sales and bounties go back into the wallet. Most players join over day 1 (sniper, seller in hour 1;
  locker, racer and the strains at launch). The strains act every 8-24 generations (agents/run.mjs) on a team budget of 1 NEAR
  each unless the scenario says otherwise.
  Careful players spend at most half their PETRI per planting and try 30 spots out of every buffer before paying for a siege;
  builders/raiders/defenders/late buy 0.02 NEAR more at most twice when low. After each tide the sell allowance reopens: bots
  (seller, sniper, racer) sell first, then a random quarter of the others sell what they hold above 16 cells' price.
  The team keeper calls advance when the board is 20 generations behind or the pot holds 5 PETRI.

Chosen: reach 4, siege_x 3. Every scenario below uses them (the siege-forbidden runs swap in the v2 buffer rule).
